# Lantern Road: Glossary

Every term a player meets, defined once (onboarding spec §7, §8 and §10). Tooltips, the Codex and Old Wick will all read from one table, `js/glossary.js`. This page is generated from it, so you can review every name and line in one pass. It is also the naming list: rename a term in `js/glossary.js` and every screen that reads the glossary follows. The names are the ones you decided on 2026-11-04 (see Decided names below), even where the game still shows an older one.

- **Name:** what the screen calls it.
- **Line:** the tooltip card. One sentence, 14 words at most.
- **More:** what the Codex page adds under the line. Two short sentences at most.
- A condition's name is its rules-editor label, with **N** for the number you choose and **[status]**, **[kind]** or **[item]** for the choice it offers.

Nothing reads the glossary yet. A later screen build wires it into the tooltips, the Codex and Wick.

**To change it:** edit `js/glossary.js`, then run `node tools/glossary.js`. The tool checks every entry against the game's own tables, then rewrites the tables below. It fails if:
- an entry is missing or misnamed
- a line runs over 14 words
- an icon or a link doesn't exist
- a quoted number no longer matches the game
- the capitals break the rules below
- a retired name, "push" or "trinket" appears

Where the game still shows a name that you've renamed, the tool reports a pending rename instead of failing. `node tools/glossary.js --check` also fails when this page is out of date.

**The rules behind them:**
- **Hero** on every screen, never character.
- **British spelling** throughout: Defence, Armour, Draught.
- **Curly apostrophes** (’) in every name and line.
- **Title Case** for skills, statuses, items, paths, buildings and regions.
- **Sentence case** for rules, conditions, buttons and labels.

**Kept as they are:**
- Doom and Hex Ward, as the game spells them.
- Threshold, for levels only.
- Crit, on screens.
- The material names, which stay placeholders.

**Still open:**
1. **Unbroken.** The Warden's second path becomes Unbroken. That path already has a skill named Unbroken, which gives the Unbroken status, so three things would share the name. The same path's passive, Hardened, also shares its name with a status.
2. **Charnel Host and Charnel Fortress.** The renamed first path shares its first word with the last skill on the Boneward path.
3. **Two kinds of lesson.** The Masters' Hall teaches lessons too, such as Rule slot IV, alongside the Tactician's Lessons I, II and III.
4. **Home material** uses "home" for a building's own region, while the Hearth is now home. "Region material" would avoid that.
5. **Hyphenated names** keep their second part in lower case under Title Case: Will-light, Plate-born, Counter-fencer, Cure-all, Blood-hex, Moth-silk Robe, Witch-iron Sickle and a few more. They could become Will-Light and so on.
6. **Other Wicks.** With Wick gone from the recruit names, three names still share the guide's: Long Wick (a skill), the Wick Wraith (a foe) and Wick Shears (an item).

## Where the code and the docs disagree

`docs/design.md` wins where they disagree. Where design.md says nothing, the glossary follows the simulation.

1. **Splash.**
   - The tip in `js/main.js` says a blow "spills onto the hero behind".
   - The simulation (`js/sim.js`) and the skill's own tip (`js/data.js`) put it on the other front-row hero, beside the one hit.
   - The glossary says beside.
2. **The Scavenger Camp.**
   - Its unlock line in `js/data.js` promises "Gear and materials while you are away".
   - design.md §9 says it gathers materials from a region you choose.
   - The home-base spec adds a rare find: a 5% chance each hour, 30% of them gear.
   - The glossary says materials, and gives the rare find in More.
3. **The starting draught rule.**
   - design.md §4's example, and `PARTY_RULES` in `js/data.js`, use "below 30% health".
   - What the 1-3 clear actually gives is "Ally: health below 50% → Healing Draught" (`js/campaign.js`), which matches its unlock line.
   - The glossary quotes no number for it.
4. **Lesson III.** The Tactician's level 4 promises "Switch rule set: party rules can change everyone's tactics on their own" (`js/base.js`, `js/buildings.js`), but no party action does it yet. The glossary leaves it out.
5. **"Push".** The Pilgrim's Vigil path says it "keeps fatigue down over a long push" (`js/classes.js`). It should say trip. The fix waits for the rename pass, with the splash tip and the Scavenger Camp's line.
6. **design.md says nothing on these. The glossary follows the code:**
   - **Enrage** also doubles foe damage every round from round 40.
   - **Relics** never sell.
   - **The Infirmary** has a still-room that brews items.
   - **Only the idle buildings** have level 3 and 5 choices.
7. **Orientation.** design.md §3 and §11 still say every screen apart from battles is portrait. The approved onboarding spec §3 makes every screen landscape. No entry mentions either.
