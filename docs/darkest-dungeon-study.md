## 2. Why it looks so good

1. **One scene, not icons on a background.**
   - Every building has its own silhouette, lit windows and chimney smoke.
   - A landmark, the ruined manor, stands on the skyline against a lit sky.
   - Props frame the edges: a signpost, a bridge, a cart, a dead tree.
2. **Light tells the story.** Warm windows and fires sit in a cold, dark world.
3. **Depth in layers:** sky, then the far landmark, then the town, then the foreground, with smoke and mist drifting between them.
4. **Texture:** ink outlines and hatching on everything.
5. **Names come when you look.** Hovering a building outlines it and shows its name and what it does, in one line.
6. **The heroes talk.** They make remarks on their own, in speech boxes.
7. **Every screen keeps the same furniture:**
   - the crest and the estate's banner at top left
   - the roster at the right
   - money and resources at bottom left
   - Embark at bottom centre
   - the menu objects at bottom right
8. **Menus are objects, not icons:** a stained-glass window, a bell, a scroll, a chest, a book, a candle.
9. **Panels are framed:**
   - dark plates for headers, with thin rules
   - ornate corners
   - a red ✕
   - news on parchment
10. **A region gets its moment:** its own card, with a picture, a name and one line.

## 4. What stays ours

- **Pixel art,** not painted illustration.
- **The violet night and amber lantern light,** not sepia and red.
- **The idle loop, the rules and the road.**
- **Idle Heroes' clarity** for screens and the guide.
- **Nothing copied:** no art, names, characters or layouts lifted, only patterns.
