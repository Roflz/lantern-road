The Hero

Tap a hero anywhere (the party panel, Formation, the roster) to open this. The hero stands full length in an arched window; the panel beside has four tabs and two one-tap conveniences. The top frame is live: switch tabs, step through the party with the arrows, try Auto and Auto-equip.

The tabs

What changed

  • The hero is the picture: full length in a stone arch, wearing their gear, with the path’s name above and level, XP and fatigue at its foot.
  • One framed panel, four tabs (Stats, Skills, Gear, Tactics) on its header plate, each with a red dot when something waits.
  • One-tap conveniences: Auto places stat points the way the class likes (Keep or Reset after); Auto-equip fills slots with the best that fits.
  • Darkest Dungeon’s sheet: sections under plates, resistances as coloured icons with numbers.
  • The arrows by the name step through the party without going back.
  • Gone from the screen: the paragraphs and hints. A skill’s or item’s words show on a tap, in a card.

For you

  1. Auto for points: should it place them as the class likes, or ask which stat to favour?
  2. The watch preview (the hero acting out a skill) moves into the arch: tap a skill to watch it there. Keep it?
