Screen 1 · title and loading

The Title

The first thing a player sees. The road lights lantern by lantern while the game loads, then waits for a tap. You approved this; the server picker is new.

How it behaves

  • It shows while the game loads (fonts, sprites and the save) for at least a second and a half, so the lanterns have time to light.
  • One tap anywhere. A returning player goes to camp, with “While you were away” first after 5 minutes or more. A new player goes to camp, where Wick is waiting.
  • The tap also starts the sound, which browsers allow only after a tap.
  • Nothing else is on it but your server: no menu. The build tag in the corner is for play-testing.
  • Servers are a placeholder for multiplayer. For now the list lives in the game itself. S1 holds today’s save. Picking another server starts a fresh save there, and your saves on other servers stay as they were. Real servers come later: hosting, other players, guilds and leaderboards. The screen, and a save kept per server, are ready for them.

Decided

  1. Servers are numbered, with no names.
  2. A new server works now: a fresh save there, with your other saves kept. It also makes the new-player tutorial easy to replay.
