The Recruit Board

After Darkest Dungeon’s stagecoach: the board’s picture on the left, a row per recruit on the right. + hires after a short confirmation; a tap on a row opens the hero card. The same panel serves the free first pick and the guide’s paid hire. The frame is live: hover a row or an icon, tap a row or a +.

How it works

  • The recruits are the focus. Their rows sit on the panel’s ground with a thin line between; the board’s picture is dimmer, there for the place.
  • A row is the stagecoach’s: level, portrait, name, class, then the price and +. Where they stand and what they do is in the tooltip on the row; stats, gear and skills on the hero card.
  • Along the top, icons and numbers: hires left, the time to a new board, the roster. Each names itself in a tooltip.
  • + asks first: the recruit, the price, your gold before and after, Cancel and Hire. Short of gold, the after goes red and Hire greys; its tooltip says how short.
  • A locked notice is a faint empty row under the last recruit; its tooltip names the rank that opens it. The ranks themselves are the second tab.
  • Every hire ends in the reveal: the hero in the light, their name, class and level, and why they signed on.
  • The first pick is this panel with Free in the price column and Wick at its left; a tap on a row is his line on the class. The guide’s paid hire (step 14) is the ordinary board, Wick pointing at the prices.
  • Anchors (data-tut): pick-card on each row, pick-price, pick-plus (new) on each +, pick-hire on the confirmation’s Hire, pick-reveal and pick-says on the reveal; board-timer and board-upgrades (new).
