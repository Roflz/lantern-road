The Results

After every battle: who levelled, how tired they are, what came home, and where to go next. The bars fill as you watch; tap to skip to the end. The top frame is live.

The popup

How it works

  • XP bars fill from where each hero was. A level-up fills the bar, flashes, lights “Level up”, empties it and fills again; several levels roll over in turn. The badge on the face shows the new level.
  • Fatigue bars grow from before to after, with marks at 50 (Weary) and 75 (Exhausted). The number turns gold, then red, as a hero crosses them; tap it for what it means.
  • Loot pops in piece by piece; rarity shows on the frame. A first clear’s unlock shows as New. Tap anything for its card.
  • The buttons: Report (the battle’s numbers, a screen of its own), Camp, Fight again, and Next, which flies up the road to the next stage with its card open. After a loss: Report, Camp, Try again, and Farm the stage before a wall.
  • It replaces today’s level-up sheet and the report that opens on its own.
  • On a trip that keeps going this popup will be shorter, with the walk on to the next stage; that waits for the Keep going decision.
