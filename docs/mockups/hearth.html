The Hearth

The camp views with Darkest Dungeon’s patterns, in our pixel art: buildings three times the size, a terrace, the Old Beacon on the skyline, names when you look, heroes who talk. The top frame is live: drag to turn, hover a building (press and hold on a phone), tap one to open it.

The buildings, close up

Each drawn in code from one kit (stone, timber, shingles, slate, windows, lanterns), so new buildings come quickly and match.

What changed

  • The buildings are about three times the size, each with its own silhouette, lit windows and smoke from its chimney.
  • The plots: six below, either side of the fire, and eight on a terrace behind, with steps up the middle. The lower row opens first, from the fire out; then the terrace, from the steps out.
  • The Old Beacon stands on the skyline against moonlit cloud, an ember still in it.
  • Depth: mist along the foot of the terrace and over the graves; lamp posts and braziers; a dead tree and a lamp framing the buildings view.
  • The crest (a lantern in an iron ring) and the Hearth’s banner sit at top left, with you beneath.
  • The menus are things: a helm for Heroes, a war table for Tactics, a satchel for the Bag, a book for the Codex, a candle for Settings.
  • Names when you look: an outline, the name, one line of what it does. The plaques can stay, or go to when you look (the toggle under the live frame).
  • Heroes talk now and then, a line about how they are.
  • Framed panels and parchment for every sheet and notice from here on.
