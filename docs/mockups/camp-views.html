Around the Fire

Two views around one fire. Drag to turn between them; let go and the camera settles on the nearer one. The top frame is live.

The two views

What changed

  • The road is the region map’s amber road, seen from the fire: it zigzags off to the hills and the Sexton’s chapel. Tap it for the map.
  • Scenery all round: hills, the chapel and the moon on the skyline; graves along the road; the camp’s low wall, tents and a cart; a walled graveyard, a crypt and a ruined chapel beyond the buildings.
  • Gone: the Party and Buildings switch (you drag instead), the Road button (you tap the road) and the stage card.
  • Heroes, Tactics and Bag are round icons in the bottom-right ribbon, the same in both views.
  • One big button in the corner does the view’s main job. In the party view it’s Set out, with the next stage on its tag. In the buildings view it’s Collect, with what’s waiting.
  • The formation is a small grid at the left, laid out as it stands in battle. Tap a face for the hero; drag to swap places.
  • Gold and bone dust sit at top right, in bars with their icon over the end, leaving the top centre to the road’s end. + goes to where you earn more: the Toll House for gold, the Scavenger Camp for bone dust.
