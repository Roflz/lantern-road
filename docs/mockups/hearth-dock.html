The Hearth’s Dock

After Darkest Dungeon’s town: what you hold on the left, Set out in the middle, the menus on the right, each named in a tooltip. The frame is live: drag to turn round the fire, hover anything in the dock (press and hold on a phone).

What moves

  • Into the dock from the top right: gold and the materials, as icons and numbers (the pill-shaped purse goes); Settings, as the candle. Help becomes the Codex’s first page.
  • Into the dock from the bottom right: Heroes, Tactics and the Bag, as the helm, the war table and the satchel, with no labels; Set out, from the round button to the plate in the middle.
  • New in the dock: the Codex, as the book, and the Recruit Board, as a notice board, so it opens from either view. The board stays in the scene too, where Wick points at it.
  • Stays: the crest, the banner, you and the next goal at top left; the formation at top right, moved up into the purse’s place.
  • Collect no longer takes Set out’s place in the buildings view. It rises over the gold whenever something waits, in either view.
  • The + by gold opens the Toll House (or, before you have it, says where its plan comes from); the + by the materials opens the Scavenger Camp. Later they open the shop.
  • Materials show once found: Bone Dust alone in Region I. From three on they sit in two rows, and past 999 a number shortens (1.9k).
  • Moving: the lanterns on Set out sway and flicker; the coin glints and the number counts up when gold comes in; a dot or a count on a menu when something new waits there.
  • Anchors (data-tut): hearth-setout, hearth-heroes, hearth-tactics, hearth-bag, hearth-collect and hearth-formation keep their names; new: hearth-board, hearth-codex, hearth-settings, hearth-gold.
