Tactics

Every hero’s tactics, the party’s and the loadout, edited in place. The owners run down the left; the chosen one’s tactics fill a framed panel; a picker at the right changes whichever part you tapped, so nothing pops over what you’re editing. The top frame is live: switch owners, tap a “when” or a “do” and pick, step a number, light or snuff a lantern, drag a grip.

The screen

What changed

  • Landscape, in three columns: who, their tactics, and the picker. Choosing a condition or an action no longer opens a sheet over the list.
  • Each tactic is one line: grip, number, “when” → “do”, and a lantern: lit is on, dark is off.
  • No paragraphs: “Only a check” and “isn’t learned: skipped in battle” become a red chip on the action (Not equipped). A condition’s or action’s meaning shows on a long press, from the glossary.
  • Numbers step in place: the “below 50%” of a condition changes with ‹ › on the chip.
  • Locked slots fold into one line that says how many and who unlocks them (the Masters’ Hall for heroes, the Tactician for the party), not why.
  • Saved sets sit in the top right as one chip, for switching between stages.
