The Hero Card

One page, after Darkest Dungeon’s character card and docs/ui-rules.md: one big hero, plain lists on a dark ground, header plates, and names and explanations in tooltips. The frame is live; hover anything for its tooltip.

What changed from the first pass

  • The stats sit under the hero as a plain list: icon and number. Names and what each does are in tooltips.
  • Points: one Spend button, with the count on a badge. It opens a mode where each stat takes a tap, Auto places the rest, and Done keeps them. No + on every row; no points into resistances.
  • Resistances are icon and number only.
  • Gear is three bigger tall cards with the rarity frame; a piece’s name and lines are in its tooltip and its card. While trying a piece on, the stats show the change in green and red, and the Spend place shows any effect gained or lost.
  • Gone: the Effects list, the “Stands / Hits” labels, “What it does”, the fatigue words, “2 to place”, the stat lines under each card, every letter-spaced capital label and the pixel typeface.
  • The Bag and Heroes use the same rules: sorts and filters are icons with tooltips, no boxes in boxes.
