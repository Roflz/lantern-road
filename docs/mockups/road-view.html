Up the Road

Tap the road at camp and the camera flies up it. Drag along the road to look ahead or back, and tap a stage for its card. The back arrow flies you home. The top frame is live.

The road view

How it works

  • Getting there: tap the road at camp, or Set out, and the camera flies up the road to the edge of your light with that stage’s card open: one more tap to go.
  • Looking along it: drag up or down, and the view settles on the nearest stage; a flick carries it a little further. The mouse wheel steps one stage at a time. That stage’s card shows at the bottom.
  • Going home: drag back past 1-1 and the camera starts back toward the fire; let go and it flies home. The ‹ at top left does the same from anywhere.
  • A stage’s card: its foes, the level and party size it wants (green when you’re ready), and what it drops. A first clear’s unlock is marked.
  • The big button: Set out at the frontier, Farm on a lit stage. Stages beyond your light are locked.
  • What’s ahead: unlit stages show their foes as silhouettes. Bubbles mark stages whose first clear brings something new. The road runs on past the boss to the next region’s gate.
  • Regions: ‹ › beside the name fly to the next or previous region; the next stays shut until its gate opens.
