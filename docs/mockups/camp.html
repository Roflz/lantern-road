The Camp

Three ways to lay out the camp, drawn with the game’s own art. Nothing here is built yet.

A · The clearing

Recommended

The fire and the party in the middle, the way out behind them, buildings on both sides. Tap anything to open it. The first six plots fit on one screen; a bigger camp scrolls sideways, like Idle Heroes’ town.

B · Walk around the fire

Today’s ring, turned on its side. Drag to walk round; the building in front is drawn large, with its card on the right.

C · Camp and party

The party stays in view beside the camp, with fatigue at a glance and Set out at the foot.
