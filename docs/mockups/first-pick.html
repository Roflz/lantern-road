The First Lantern-Bearer

Wick sends you to the Recruit Board. Three would carry the lantern; tap each to hear about them, then hire one, free. The top frame is live.

Step by step

Wick’s lines

  1. At the board: “Three would carry the lantern. Tap each to hear about them.”
  2. Sellsword: “Hits hardest, up front. Simple and strong.”
  3. Hexblade: “Curses that tick. Patient, and hard on healers.”
  4. Pilgrim: “Heals, and burns the dead. Slow, but hard to kill.”
  5. Back at camp: “A bearer needs a road. Set out when you’re ready.”

How it works

  • Only the board takes a tap until you’ve hired; the rest of the camp waits. This is the forced first session.
  • The cards say only who they are, where they stand and what they do, in icons. Wick says the rest.
  • Hiring is free for this first hero. The two you didn’t pick come back on the board later, with the free recruit at 1-2.
  • The reveal is a moment, as in Idle Heroes: the hero in the light, their rolled name, class and level. Tap anywhere to go on.
  • Afterwards the board shows its usual face, with new faces from 1-2.
