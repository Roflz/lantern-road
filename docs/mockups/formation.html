Formation

Tap the party panel in the party view to open it. Your party stands as it does in battle, front on the right, facing the next stage’s foes, with the roster along the bottom. The top frame is live: drag heroes onto places, swap them, or drag one down to rest.

The screen

What it does

  • Opens from the party panel in the party view. Its first visit is step 12 of the first session, when the second hero joins.
  • The line is the party as it stands in battle: two places in front, four behind, facing the next stage’s foes. It’s the same grid as the party panel, only larger, with the heroes standing in it.
  • Drag a portrait onto a place, a hero onto another to swap them, or a hero down to the roster to rest them. Tap a portrait for the hero (screen 8).
  • Auto fills empty places with rested heroes, front-row classes in front, the highest level first. Done closes it.
  • Warnings, not rules: a melee hero in the back only Defends (that’s how battles work today), and a Spent hero is greyed. The Oathbound Gorget still keeps its wearer in front.
  • Words: Front, Back, the count, levels. The rows are explained once, by Wick, on the first visit.
