# The Story

The story bible. You approved the spine on 2026-11-05 ("A, the Fallen Kingdom"). The first session, Wick's lines, the region cards and the rename pass work from it. The earlier options, and why they were dropped, are in `docs/story-options.md`.

**Status:** approved on 2026-11-05. The game's working title is **The Lost Kingdom** (see the end).

**How much story:** a small one, as in Idle Heroes. It's just enough for the opening and the game loop to make sense. The player has no backstory; the heroes carry the colour.

## In one line

You run a company of heroes for hire. Clear the old road through the lost kingdom, all the way to its dead king, and keep what you clear.

## The spine

- **The world:** beyond a wall lies the old kingdom, which fell fifty years ago. One old road runs from the gate in the wall to its capital.
- **What went wrong:**
  - Its king wanted to rule forever. So he learned to raise the dead, and made sure he'd rise when he died.
  - He did rise, but he came back hollow: cold, empty, and still wanting to rule.
  - His people fled, so he raised their dead to serve him.
  - The army sent to stop him burned at the Ashen Keep.
  - The living walled the kingdom off and left it to the dead.
- **The dead come back as they died.** The drowned still drip, and the burned still smoulder. That's all the magic a player needs to know.
- **You:** the head of a company of heroes for hire.
  - The land is no use to anyone as it is. So the crown on this side of the wall has a standing offer: clear the road, and keep what you clear.
  - You've signed. You hire the heroes and send them out, but you don't fight.
- **What you want:** to clear the road all the way to the throne, and keep it.
- **In the way:** the dead, and one of the king's lords or allies in each region. The dead won't lie down while the king sits on his throne.
- **The end:** the king falls and the dead lie down. The land is the company's, and the camp has grown into a town.

## Rules that stay rules

These get no story, as in Idle Heroes. Nobody in the game explains them:
- heroes who fall get back up after a battle
- fatigue and rest
- recruiting and the Recruit Board
- tactics, gear, skills and levels

The story touches the systems only where it fits on its own:
- **The Hearth** is the old inn at the gate. Your company camps round its fire and builds the camp up.
- **Tolls:** the road you've cleared is yours, so travellers pay to use it, even while you're away.
- **Loot:** the old kingdom is full of what people left behind, and of what the dead were buried with.
- **Recruits** come to the gate looking for work, as they have for fifty years.
- **Renown** is your company's name, and how far it has spread.

## Wick, the guide

- **Who he is:** the old man who keeps the Hearth, the inn at the gate.
  - He was born in the old kingdom, and fled through the gate as a boy when it fell.
  - He has kept the inn's fire ever since, and watched fifty years of companies go through. Most came back early. Some didn't come back.
- **Why he helps:** he wants to go home. He knows the road: he walked it as a boy, and he has heard every company's story since.
- **His voice:**
  - warm, dry and a little tired, in short, plain sentences
  - he's seen a lot of companies and isn't easily impressed, but he's kind and on your side
  - he understates danger
  - he never mocks the player, and never stays gloomy for more than a line
- **On the road:** at each new region, he remembers it as it was. His lines are in the table below.
- **His name:** he keeps the name Wick. He keeps an inn now, not lamps.

## The road: regions and bosses

The road runs from the gate to the old capital. Each boss holds its stretch of road for the king.

| Region | What it is | The boss, and its part | Wick, on arrival |
|---|---|---|---|
| I Gravefields | The old kingdom's burial grounds, just past the gate | **The Sexton** is the king's gravedigger, still alive and still digging. He opens the old graves so the dead can rise for the king, and keeps whatever was buried with them. His diggers and hired mourners work for a share. | "The old kingdom buried its dead here. They don't stay buried." |
| II Drowned Chapel | The chapel where the road crosses the river | **The Drowned Bishop** crowned the king. He drowned with his flock when the river took the chapel, and the king raised them all. They hold the crossing for him. | "The choir here was famous. They still sing. Don't stop to listen." |
| III Thornwood | The old wood, which has swallowed the road | **The Antlered One** is the god of the wood, older than the kingdom. It isn't the king's, but it's glad of him: with the living gone, nobody cuts its trees. It keeps the road shut. | "When I was a boy, you could see the road through here." |
| IV Ashen Keep | The fortress that guards the way to the capital | **The Burnt Marshal** is the king's marshal. He held the keep against the army sent to stop the king, then burned it with the army inside. The king raised them all, the marshal too, and they hold it still. | "The army got this far. No further." |
| V Hollow Throne | The old capital, and the king's court of the dead | **The Hollow King** wanted to rule forever, and he's still at it, with a court of the dead. | "I was born three streets from here. It looked better then." |

## The ending

1. The Hollow King falls. This time he stays down: there's nothing left of him to raise.
2. With nobody holding them up, the dead lie down, all across the old kingdom.
3. Under the crown's offer, the land is the company's. Settlers come through the gate, and the Hearth grows into a town.
4. Wick walks up the road to the house he was born in, and he's back at the inn by nightfall. "Smaller than I remembered. And someone has to keep the fire."

Play goes on after the ending: farming, higher levels, and anything added later.

## Tone and voice

1. **The world is played straight.** Its rules never joke. The dead are dangerous, and the fall was sad.
2. **The humour is in how people talk.** It's dry, understated, and matter-of-fact about grim things.
3. **Spooky but cosy, and never grim for long.** A practical line follows a sad one. The fire, food, rest and company are the cosy half.
4. **Plain words.** No made-up words, puns, winks at the player or modern slang. Keep a bubble to about 8–12 words.
5. **The heroes are the story.** Each class has its own reason to sign on, and its own voice. These feed the Recruit Board and the heroes' remarks.

| Class | Why they sign on, in their own words |
|---|---|
| Sellsword | "Someone's paying. That's all I need to know." |
| Hexblade | "I curse things for a living. Over there, everything's earned one." |
| Pilgrim | "The dead deserve their rest. I'm here to give it to them." |
| Warden | "Somebody has to stand at the front." |
| Lamplighter | "It's dark over that wall. Somebody should bring a lamp." |
| Crossbowman | "Paid by the bolt, and there's plenty to aim at." |
| Duellist | "Nobody's beaten the Burnt Marshal. Yet." |
| Alchemist | "The king's magic is made of something. I'd like to know what." |
| Barber-Surgeon | "Companies need patching. I need paying." |

| Gravecaller | "Same magic as the king. I just give them back after." |
| Cutpurse | "A whole kingdom, and nobody guarding it." |

**Some of Wick's lines:**
- Greeting a new company: "Another company. Come in. Sit by the fire."
- When a tired hero comes home: "Let them sit. The road will keep."
- Before the Sexton: "He's dug graves here for sixty years. Now he digs them up."
- After a loss: "Not your day. Have a look at the hint, then try again."
- When a recruit is hired: "Another one for the road. Feed them first."
- When the Toll House is built: "Travellers have heard the road's open. They'll pay to use it."

## The opening

Wick tells the story at the fire before your first tap. Each line takes one tap, and Skip jumps to the name box. It needs no new art, though four pictures could come later.

**Wick's welcome:**
1. "Another company. Come in. Sit by the fire."
2. "I'm Wick. I keep the inn here, at the gate."

**The story, in four beats:**

3. "Fifty years ago, the king over that wall wanted to rule forever."
4. "So he learned to raise the dead. When he died, he rose again, hollow."
5. "His people fled, so he raised their dead to serve him. We walled him in."
6. "Now the crown has an offer: clear the old road, and keep what you clear."

**Then:**

7. "Who's in charge?" (the name box: with no name given, you're "Captain")
8. "[Name]. Welcome to the Hearth. Your company can camp here."
9. On the Recruit Board: "First, you'll need heroes. The Recruit Board's there."

**Later in the first session.** These are drafts for the steps in `docs/onboarding-spec.md` §6.4:
- **Step 2, the first pick:** "Three looking for work. Tap each to hear about them."
- **Step 4, setting out:** "The road starts at the gate. Take [hero] through."
- **Step 5, the road:** "Each stage is a stretch of road. Win its fight, and the stretch is yours." Then, on Set out: "Clear the first."
- **Back on the road after 1-1's first clear:** "First stretch cleared. It's yours now."
- **Step 12, the goodbye:** "The Sexton's at the end of the Gravefields. Plenty of road before him." Then: "I'll be by the fire."

**The test** (from `docs/tutorial-study.md`): after the first session, a new player can say:
- who they are: the head of a company
- what they're doing: clearing the road to the dead king, and keeping it
- how: hiring heroes and sending them down the road, a stage at a time
- why to come back: the heroes rest, and the next stretch waits. From 1-5, the road earns tolls while they're away.

**Art:**
- The lantern art needs new subjects: the title screen, the crest, the region card's emblem and the stage markers. The gate and the inn's fire are the natural ones.

- The Old Beacon on the hill can be the wall's old warning beacon, as scenery.
